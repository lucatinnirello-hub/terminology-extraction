Extract

Finding and scoring candidate terms

7 nodes
#Node and typeWhat it doesSetup you do
21 Stage 1 statistical extractionCode Takes the source-language side of each TMX unit, the source text of each XLIFF unit (skipping TM matches and units marked do-not-translate), or sentences from text. Skips XLIFF files in a different source language. Removes duplicate segments, and scores every 1–4 word phrase with YAKE's features. Then merges near-duplicates, drops sub-phrases that rarely appear on their own, and keeps the top N terms with example segments. None
22 Stage 1 rowsCode One row per term for the spreadsheet.
Can stop the job No candidate terms (usually the wrong source language)
 None
23 Stage 1 spreadsheetConvert to File Builds stage1_candidates_….xlsx. Kept in memory until the upload at the end. None
24 Stage 2 NLP stubCode Placeholder for future lemmatising (valve / valves) and dropping verb phrases. Passes terms through unchanged for now. None
25 Match translationsCode Only with translations on. Reads the target side of the TMX and XLIFF files and suggests a translation for each term: the target phrase found in the translations of the term's segments and rarely elsewhere, built from the translations of its words. Rates how consistently the files use it (high, medium, low) and lists other translations used. No AI. None
26 Check restricted listCode The compliance gate. AI review is allowed only if it was requested, client approval was ticked, and the client is not on the restricted list written into this node. None
27 AI review approved?If Allowed: terms go to the AI review. Not allowed: straight to Terms to items (no AI). None

35 Glossary termsCode Wildcards-only jobs. Reads the glossary, finds the target-term column (named on the page, or from the language name), and lists each target term with its source term. Several terms in one cell, separated by ";", are handled one by one; wildcards already there are worked out again. TMX/XLIFF files in the job only supply their target-language words, to spot false hits.
